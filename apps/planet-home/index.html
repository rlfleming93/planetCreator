How a week turns into a planet

Every session leaves something behind on the planet, and the week as a whole sets its sea and its colours.

  1. 1

    It happens in your browser

    Your .fit files, or the whole export Garmin emails you, are read on your own machine. No account, nothing uploaded, and the weeks you keep stay on your shelf in this browser.

  2. 2

    Every session raises its own thing

    A run raises a ridge, and a longer run draws a longer one. A ride carves a valley, a swim digs a lagoon, lifting stands up rock, and yoga plants a grove.

  3. 3

    The week sets the sea and the colours

    The week's sweat fills the sea, the temperature your watch saw picks the colours, and hard work pushes them toward red rock. A race leaves a monument at the finish, and its course is painted red.

  4. 4

    Then you land on it

    Walk the planet, and keep it on your shelf: one planet for each week, laid out around the sun, a year to a lap. Print a week as a poster, or copy a link that leaves the names behind.
