There are no clips here yet. The computer that syncs the weeks makes them with bun scripts/story-render.mjs --both.

The week so far and the last full week, painted after each sync. Share opens the phone's share sheet with the clip, for Instagram Stories, TikTok or X, and copies the caption to paste in. Story is the tall one for Stories and TikTok. Square and wide sit better in a feed. On an iPhone the sheet's Save Video puts the clip in Photos, and Add to Home Screen keeps this page a tap away.
