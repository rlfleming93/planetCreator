Bring your week

Most watches save each workout as a .fit file. Pick a week of them and this page paints that week as a planet you can land on. Your files are read here, in your browser, and never uploaded.

Strava or files

Garmin will ask you to let Junction read your Garmin Connect data. Junction is how this site reaches Garmin. Your last full week paints itself once Garmin sends it, which can take a few minutes. Tap Disconnect, or wait 30 days, and Planet Creator has Junction delete it.

Garmin, through Junction

Another week

Painting asks Planet Creator's Cloudflare Function to read the week from Junction. The Function turns it into a planet in memory and keeps none of it. Junction and Cloudflare process the request.

Connect with Strava

Strava will ask to let this site read your activities, private ones too, so no session is left off the planet. Leave both boxes ticked, and your last full week paints itself when you're back. Nothing is stored on Planet Creator's server.

Strava caps how many athletes a new app can connect until it reviews the app. If Strava shows a limit error, use files for now.

Your weeks

Painting asks Planet Creator's Cloudflare Function to fetch your weeks from Strava. The Function turns them into planets in memory and doesn't store them. Strava and Cloudflare process the request.

Planet Creator no longer has a token to revoke. Revoke it in your Strava settings.

    Getting the files

    Garmin, on a phone
    1. Open connect.garmin.com in your phone's browser and sign in. The Garmin Connect app can't export files.
    2. Open a workout from the week, tap the gear icon, then Export Original (some layouts say Export File). A .zip lands in your downloads.
    3. Do the same for the week's other workouts.
    4. Come back here, tap Choose files and pick the .zip files. On an iPhone they're in the Files app, under Downloads.
    Garmin, on a computer
    1. The same Export Original works on connect.garmin.com, one workout at a time. Drop the .zip files here without unzipping them.
    2. For every week at once, open Export Your Data in your Garmin account and request the export. Garmin emails a link to a .zip, often within two days. Drop the whole .zip here.
    Strava

    Connecting Strava straight to this page isn't switched on yet. Its exports work:

    1. One workout: open it on strava.com, open its ⋯ menu and pick Export Original. If it was recorded as a .fit, that's the file. GPX and TCX files won't work here.
    2. Every week: open Download or Delete Your Account on strava.com and pick Request Your Archive. Strava emails a .zip, usually within a few hours. Pick that .zip here.
    3. Strava only has what synced to it. A week from before you linked your watch needs the watch's own files.
    Coros, Suunto, Wahoo or Polar

    Their apps and websites export .fit files too, and those work the same way.

    Apple Watch

    Apple Health's own export has no .fit files in it, so it can't be read here. An app that saves your workouts as .fit files, such as HealthFit, works.
