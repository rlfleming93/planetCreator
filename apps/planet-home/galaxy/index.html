    Marks on the weeks

    • A race: a rose shell of light.
    • Most miles yet: a star with two beams.
    • Most hours yet: a warm ball of old stars.
    • First heavy week after the longest gap: a small galaxy torn into a stream.
    • Steady streak: a dark dust lane, 6+ weeks of 5+ sessions and 5+ hours.
    • Busy streak: pink nebulae, 4+ weeks of 5+ sessions.
